# Continuum Companion

*Explore the Human Side of Healing.*

Continuum Companion is a modular, locally hosted, open-source AI wellness and research platform designed to bridge Eastern medicinal traditions and Western medical knowledge in a transparent, educational, and supportive way.

The platform is built to work alongside licensed medical care — never replace it.

Continuum Companion combines AI-assisted research, source-backed question and answer systems, wellness planning tools, research archiving, and integrative educational frameworks into a privacy-focused self-hosted platform.

The system is released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)** to ensure transparency, openness, community collaboration, and long-term accessibility.

---

# Core Philosophy

Continuum Companion is designed around several core principles:

- Supportive, not diagnostic
