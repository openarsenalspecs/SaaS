## Overview

Corelia is an open-source, AI-powered, sovereign content management system (CMS) specification designed to provide a complete digital publishing platform that is secure, intelligent, extensible, and privacy-first.

Unlike traditional CMS platforms, Corelia is designed around an AI-first architecture with end-to-end encryption, modular services, modern APIs, and complete ownership of user data. Every major subsystem is designed as an independent module that can be enabled, disabled, replaced, or extended without affecting the rest of the platform.

Corelia serves as a complete platform for websites, blogs, documentation portals, e-commerce stores, communities, knowledge bases, intranets, digital asset management, enterprise publishing, and AI-driven applications.
