# Mindora

**Mindora – From Lessons to Real-World Discovery**

Mindora is an **open-source AI-powered education platform** designed to provide personalized, adaptive, and source-verified learning experiences across all subjects. It combines artificial intelligence, interactive learning tools, global and local customization, and real-world discovery to create a flexible educational ecosystem for learners of all ages.

Mindora is built with a **modular architecture**, allowing the core platform to provide essential educational capabilities while optional plug-in modules extend functionality for specialized subjects, regions, industries, and learning experiences.

The platform is designed around transparency, accessibility, and evidence-based education. Every AI-generated answer must provide proof of source through citations or references, ensuring learners can verify information and develop strong research skills.

---

# Core Philosophy

Mindora is built on the principles of:

- **Personalized Learning** — Every learner receives adaptive lessons based on knowledge level, progress, interests, and learning goals.
- **Source-Verified Education** — AI-generated responses must provide supporting sources whenever providing factual information.
- **Real-World Discovery** — Learning extends beyond the screen through locations, experiences, and exploration.
- **Global Accessibility** — Education adapts to local languages, cultures, regions, and educational needs.
- **Open Source Development** — The platform encourages transparency, collaboration, and community-driven expansion.

---

# Plugin Development

Developers can extend Mindora through modular plug-ins.

Plugins should:

- Maintain AGPL-3.0+ compatibility
- Provide source verification
- Follow accessibility guidelines
- Maintain educational accuracy standards

# Security & Content Rules

Mindora does not support user-uploaded attachments.

The platform uses:

- Verified internal content
- Structured educational databases
- Approved external references
- Text-based community contributions

This ensures consistency, security, and reliability.
